# The Phase 1 paper

Sources for the paper on Phase 1: label-efficient crop-type segmentation with frozen geospatial foundation models,
and its spatial transferability across countries and agro-ecological zones. The paper reports what
[../protocol.md](../protocol.md), [../pipeline.md](../pipeline.md), [../results_estonia/REPORT.md](../results_estonia/REPORT.md)
and the transfer plan ([../../research/transferability/transfer_setup.md](../../research/transferability/transfer_setup.md))
establish, and nothing they do not.
