Spatial transferability: experimental set-up

Agreed with the user on 5 and 6 November 2026. The alternatives considered and the evidence behind each choice are in transfer_plan.md ↗. AlphaEarth is left out for now.

The experiment trains one model in every country and one in every agro-ecological zone, each on the same amount of labels, and tests every model in every other country and zone. How much a model loses away from home indicates whether transfer is limited by national borders or by climate.

The questions

QuestionMatrix
Q1How much does a model lose in a country it was not trained in?country × country
Q2Does the loss follow climate? Is it smaller within a GAEZ zone than across zones, and which climate differences does it track once distance is accounted for?country × country, zone × zone
Q3Does a model trained on a whole zone, across national borders, hold up within that zone, and can it replace a national model?zone × zone, country × zone
Q4Do the frozen foundation models lose less than the U-Net trained from scratch?all three

Countries and zones

The study covers 19 countries with labels for 2021: AT, BE (Flanders, Wallonia), BG, CZ, DE (Brandenburg, Lower Saxony, North Rhine-Westphalia), DK, EE, ES and FR (mainland), FI, IE, IT (Tuscany), LT, LV, NL, PT (mainland), SE, SI and SK. The labels come from EuroCrops v2 where a region has 2021, and from v11 otherwise (EE, LV, LT, SE, Lower Saxony).

The zones are the GAEZ v5 agro-ecological zones (33 classes at 1 km), used exactly as GAEZ maps them. A class describes a climate, as a thermal regime crossed with a moisture regime, unless a soil, terrain or irrigation condition applies, in which case that condition names the class (a special class). Sample strategy

  1. All 46 million parcels of the 2021 labels are summed per block (4 × 4 chips, 8.96 km) before any imagery is downloaded.
  2. A quarter of all blocks, fixed by position, are test blocks and the rest are training blocks, for every model alike, so that no model trains on a chip on which any model is tested. As in the main workflow, a 1,600 m buffer separates test land from training land.
  3. Every model receives the same amount of labels: 250,000 ha of labelled crop land for training and 37,500 ha for validation. A test set holds at least 50 blocks and at least 75,000 ha. The number of chips therefore depends on how densely a domain is labelled, from 805 to 4,962 training chips with a median of about 1,200.
  4. Each sample is spread evenly over its domain and chosen so that its crop mix matches that of the domain. Training samples deviate from it by 0.1 to 3.0 % and test samples by 0.2 to 4.9 %, and no crop is more than 2.1 points off.
  5. For training, a zone sample reuses the blocks drawn for the countries wherever they fall within the zone's own spread, so that each zone keeps its actual mix of countries; test sets are always drawn independently. Because a block usually contains two or three GAEZ classes, a zone sample consists of the zone's chips within the drawn blocks.

A model learns a species where the labels name it and only the group where they do not, as for Lithuania's "unspecified cereals", which cover 46 % of its labelled land. Land off the list is ignored in the same way as unlabelled land; this concerns forest, land declared as "unknown" and crops that fail the rule, such as rice, flax and poppy. The mapping of every HCAT name is in crop_classes.csv.

Models and training

Four foundation models remain frozen, each with a trained decoder, as in the main workflow: TerraMind v1 large and THOR v1 large on Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1, Prithvi-EO-2.0 600M on Sentinel-2, and TESSERA v2 Medium on Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1. Only the decoder learns. The baseline is a U-Net trained from scratch on Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1, in which every weight is learnt. Each model trains for 45 epochs, and the checkpoint of lowest validation loss on the model's own domain is kept. The 31 domains (19 countries and 12 zones) and five models give 155 training runs, first with one seed and, for the final run, with three.

Scores

The main score is Macro-F1 per pixel, with Macro-F1 per parcel beside it. The main readout counts the species with support on both sides, that is, at least 30 parcels in the training sample and at least 30 parcels in at least 5 test blocks. A second readout counts every species of the test set and scores 0 for a species the model never saw; both readouts are repeated at the group level. The home score is the score of a domain's own model on its own test set, and the loss of a model on a test set is

loss = 1 − (its score) / (home score of the test set's own model)

A loss of 0 means that nothing is lost, and 0.30 a score 30 % below that of the test domain's own model; the absolute drop is reported alongside. Every score receives an interval by resampling test blocks.

The matrices we will get

Each matrix is produced per model, per pixel and per parcel, as scores and as losses. ◆ marks a home score, · a zero-shot score, and ◐ a cell where the zone model has trained on that country's land and is therefore not zero-shot.

Cost and status

The data amount to 65,139 chips, 49,373 for the countries and 15,766 more for the zones, or about 680 GB of Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1. The country samples have been downloading since 5 November 2026, with Estonia and Latvia mostly on disk already; at the archive's pace the full set takes about two weeks, the zones following the countries. Masks are rasterised once the imagery is in. Training comprises 155 runs per seed on 800 to 5,000 chips each, roughly 100 to 150 GPU-hours on the cluster.

AlphaEarth remains open, since its source tiles amount to up to 1.6 TB. Other years, and a source country combined with a few target labels, are kept for later.

Files

All in this folder, run from the repository root with poetry run python docs/research/transferability/<script>: label_blocks.py (labels per block), draw_samples.py (the samples and their figures), crop_classes.py (the crop classes), zone_pools.py and aez_maps.py (the zones and their maps), gaez_metrics.py (the climate metrics). The export of the drawn chips: scripts/hub/build_sample_chips.py, into data/eurocrops_chips/<CC>_2021_transfer/.

Source: the research note transfer_setup.md, agreed on 5 and 6 November 2026; not yet in the protocol.

3. Experiments that justify the pipeline

Each experiment varies one factor and compares the variants fit by fit, on shared draws and seeds. Experiments 1 to 6 were done on the crop segmentation only pipeline, before the crop segmentation + border pipeline existed. Each decided a part both pipelines share (the inputs, the frozen backbones and their saved features, frozen encoders), so their decisions carry over and none is rerun; experiments 1 to 3 ran on TerraMind or THOR with Sentinel-2 alone. Experiments 7 to 9 designed the crop segmentation + border pipeline.

Crop segmentation only pipeline

1
REPORT.md ↗

THOR token size: 160, 80 or 40 m

Done on the crop segmentation only pipeline

THOR v1 large on Sentinel-2, 733-chip sample, three seeds, 15 epochs.

Result. 40 m beats 160 m by about 0.05 Macro-F1 at every K and wins all nine paired fits; its cache is 16 times larger, about 25.5 TB for Estonia.

Decision. 160 m kept, for parity with TerraMind's 14 × 14 grid and a cache that fits a cluster node.

2
REPORT.md ↗

Rotations: 1, 4 or 8 D4 copies

Done on the crop segmentation only pipeline

TerraMind v1 large on Sentinel-2, all of EE_2021_rev, three seeds, 15 epochs.

Result. Eight copies beat the identity by 0.047, 0.061 and 0.070 at K = 100, 20 and 5 %; four sit between. Most of the gain comes through the checkpoint.

Decision. Training chips are cached in all eight flipped and rotated copies.

3
REPORT.md ↗

Temporal grid: 12 monthly or 24 half-month frames

Done on the crop segmentation only pipeline

TerraMind v1 large on Sentinel-2, 733-chip sample, three seeds, 15 epochs.

Result. +0.015, +0.035 and +0.001 for 24 frames, all within the seed spread, at twice the encoding cost; in late October 73 % of the chips have no image at all.

Decision. Twelve monthly composites.

4
REPORT.md ↗

TESSERA: its encoder on our composites, or the store's embeddings

Done on the crop segmentation only pipeline

Probe on EE_2021_rev_mini; fits on all of EE_2021_rev, three seeds.

Result. The encoder on our composites matches the store in a probe (0.371 against 0.373). In the main workflow frozen v2 is 0.018, 0.037 and 0.068 below the store arm.

Decision. TESSERA v2, frozen, on our composites: no 0.7 px offset, the same imagery as every arm.

5
REPORT.md ↗

TerraMind: Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1 or both

Done on the crop segmentation only pipeline

TerraMind v1 large, all of EE_2021_rev, three seeds, 15 epochs.

Result. Both sensors beat Sentinel-2 alone by 0.026, 0.054 and 0.025 and win every fit; Sentinel-1 alone is 0.02 to 0.07 below Sentinel-2.

Decision. Every model reads every sensor it was pretrained on.

6
REPORT.md ↗

Fine-tuned or frozen encoder

Done on the crop segmentation only pipeline

Every encoder with weights, all of EE_2021_rev, 45 epochs, one seed.

Result. TESSERA gains 0.04 to 0.08; TerraMind, THOR and Prithvi about 0.01 in validation, their test difference set by the checkpoint. Fine-tuning costs 4 to 15 times the frozen fit.

Decision. Encoders stay frozen in the main workflow; fine-tuning is reported as an insight.

Crop segmentation + border pipeline

7
REPORT.md ↗

PRUE on TESSERA: field borders from a frozen embedding
